WiiConverted builds a Windows version of the Wii game Rabbids Go Home from files you own, and gives it a launcher: Play, the mods and the settings in one window. The setup converts your Wii disc's game data and runs it on the PC release's program; your original files are only read.

You need

  • The Wii disc's game data: the folder with RGH.BF, RGH.wii.sns.BF and RGH.$hd$.bik.BF, extracted from your own disc.
  • The PC release of Rabbids Go Home, installed or copied: its executable, shaders folder, binkw32.dll and archive.
  • About 2.5 GB of free space for the game folder.

The setup, step by step

  1. WELCOMEUnzip the download and run WiiConverted Setup.exe. The steps are listed at the left of its window.
  2. 1WII FILESPick the folder with your Wii disc's files: RGH.BF and the sound and video archives beside it.
  3. 2PC RELEASEPick the folder of the PC release of Rabbids Go Home, installed or copied.
  4. 3DESTINATIONPick an empty folder, or an earlier game folder to refresh it. Your Wii save can come along.
  5. 4CONVERTThe Wii archive is converted into the game folder. This takes a few minutes.
  6. 5DONEGo Home: the window becomes the game folder's launcher, with Play on its Home page and the mods a click away.
