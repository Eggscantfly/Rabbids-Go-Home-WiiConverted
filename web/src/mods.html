{{count}} How to install a mod

Mods for the launcher's Mods page. A mod is a folder in the game folder's mods folder: a mod.json (name, author, version, description, icon, screenshots) and what the game reads instead of its archive - entries, records, main.lua. Nothing in the game's archive is changed: turn a mod off and the game has its own files back.

{{cards}}

Installing a mod

  1. Download the mod and unzip it into the mods folder of your game folder, so that its mod.json ends up in mods\<the mod>\. The launcher's Mods page has a button that opens that folder.
  2. In the launcher, open Mods, click the mod and press Enable.
  3. Press Play. The game reads the enabled mods from their folders when it starts; Disable takes a mod out again.
